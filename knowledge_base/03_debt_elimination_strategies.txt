# FINANCIAL KNOWLEDGE BASE: DEBT ELIMINATION & CREDIT STRATEGIES

## The Two Primary Debt Paydown Methodologies

### 1. The Debt Avalanche Method (Mathematically Optimal)
- How it works:
  1. List all debts ordered by Annual Percentage Rate (APR) from highest interest rate to lowest interest rate, regardless of balance size.
  2. Pay the minimum monthly payment on all debts to protect credit score and avoid late penalties.
  3. Channel 100% of extra discretionary debt payoff funds directly into the balance with the single highest APR (typically credit cards carrying 20%-29% APR).
  4. Once that debt is fully extinguished, roll the entire monthly payment forward into the next highest interest rate loan.
- Benefits: Minimizes total interest paid over time, leading to the fastest mathematical debt freedom date. Recommended for analytical, disciplined savers.

### 2. The Debt Snowball Method (Behavioral & Psychological Momentum)
- How it works:
  1. List all debts ordered by total outstanding balance from smallest balance to largest balance, regardless of APR.
  2. Maintain minimum required payments on all debts.
  3. Throw all extra debt paydown cash at the smallest outstanding balance until it reaches $0.
  4. Celebrate the milestone and roll the entire cash commitment into the second-smallest debt.
- Benefits: Produces rapid early psychological victories, building behavioral momentum and self-efficacy for borrowers who feel overwhelmed by multiple accounts.

## Healthy Debt-to-Income (DTI) Benchmarks
- Total Debt-to-Income Ratio = (Total Monthly Debt Payments / Gross Monthly Income) * 100
- Under 20%: Excellent financial flexibility.
- 20% to 35%: Healthy, standard consumer level.
- 36% to 43%: Caution zone; mortgage lenders often require DTI <= 43% for qualified mortgages.
- Above 43%: Severe debt burden; priority intervention required to curtail interest expenses.

## Credit Card APR Hazard
Consumer credit card APRs frequently range from 18% to 30%. Carrying revolving balances at these rates compounds exponentially against wealth accumulation. Paying off high-interest debt represents a guaranteed, risk-free, tax-free return equal to the loan's interest rate (e.g. paying off a 24% credit card equals an unbeatable guaranteed 24% return).
